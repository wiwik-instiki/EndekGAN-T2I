# EndekGAN-T2I: IEEE study reproducibility package

The original repository notebooks and archived generated examples remain unchanged.

The integrated evidence workflow is in [research_reproducibility_2026](research_reproducibility_2026/README.md), including the master notebook, source notebook provenance, code, aggregate results and grayscale figures.

**Start:** Open research_reproducibility_2026/00_MASTER_ENDekGAN_EXPERIMENT_PIPELINE.ipynb in VS Code; the default performs safe numerical re-analysis (21 completed archived runs). Full model training is separate, expensive, and requires separately authorized image data.

Research limitations and dataset/licensing restrictions are detailed in [DATA_RIGHTS.md](research_reproducibility_2026/docs/DATA_RIGHTS.md). No raw Endek dataset, model weights or private expert ratings are published with this package.
